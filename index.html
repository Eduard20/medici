A SMALL NOTE

Different sounds.
The same feeling.

A recording is already a finished performance. To replay its notes on another instrument, this studio needs a MIDI arrangement: a file containing the notes and timing.

The included “Love for Duty” MIDI is a piano-score transcription from your provided sheet music. It uses a steady 120 BPM. Free slowdowns, fermatas, and pedal are not reproduced by this player, so it differs from the original performance. You can also import your own arrangement. The studio renders the melodic notes in your chosen instrument. It isn’t an AI audio converter, and it doesn’t remove vocals or recreate every performance detail.
