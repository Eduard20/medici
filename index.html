A SMALL NOTE
Music, with a
little imagination.
This studio reads printed sheet music with experimental optical recognition. The result is a rough draft: notes, rhythm, accidentals, and multiple voices can be misread. Repeats, expressive timing, and pedal aren’t fully reproduced. Please check what you hear.
Your PDF and recognized notes stay on your device. Instrument samples and the recognition engine need an internet connection. Your converted scores and playback settings are remembered only in this browser.
Credits, source & privacy
Application code is AGPL-3.0. Third-party notices. PDF recognition uses Partsong/homr locally. Engine and model files load from jsDelivr and official Partsong hosting after you choose a PDF. These hosts receive ordinary request metadata, but no PDF or note data.
Samples: FluidR3_GM by Frank Wen and contributors, via midi-js-soundfonts, CC BY 3.0. Samples load from gleitz.github.io when requested. Playback libraries: @tonejs/midi and soundfont-player (MIT). Fonts: DM Sans and Playfair Display from Google Fonts. No paid service, account, or soundtrack audio is included.
Recognized scores use IndexedDB. No cloud backup or cross-device sync is provided. Open a piece and use “Remove saved score” to delete that saved copy. Pitch bend, sustain-pedal automation, and percussion parts are not played by this melodic instrument studio.
